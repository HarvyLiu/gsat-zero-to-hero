## Instructions

This is an **original** practice paper: every item was written for this site, and none is a CEEC exam item. The layout is based on the five 111–115 Math A papers; **the 116 layout has not been published and is not confirmed**.

| Part | Item type | Items | Count | Points each | Subtotal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Part 1 | Single choice | 1–6 | $6$ | $5$ | $30$ |
| Part 1 | Multiple select | 7–12 | $6$ | $5$ | $30$ |
| Part 1 | Fill-in | 13–17 | $5$ | $5$ | $25$ |
| Part 2 | Mixed set | 18–20 | $3$ | $3+6+6$ | $15$ |

The points and types of the sub-items in the mixed set vary from year to year. This paper uses $3$ (single choice) $+6+6$, the same split as the 111, 112 and 114 Math A papers.

Rules for this paper:

- **Time:** $100$ minutes. Time yourself, and don't look anything up or check the answers along the way.
- **No calculator.** The exam-room rule: <Fact id="exam.calculators" />
- **Single choice** scores only when correct; a wrong answer, a blank or more than one mark scores zero.
- **Multiple select:** <Fact id="exam.multi-select-scoring" />
- **Fill-in:** answer cell by cell in the boxes the item gives (①②③…), each cell holding one digit $0$ to $9$ or a minus sign “−”. The item scores only if every cell is correct, and a wrong answer costs nothing. For example, if the answer is $-12$ and the item gives three cells ①②③, fill in “−”, $1$, $2$ in that order.
- **Items 19 and 20 are written items:** write out your full working or reasons on paper, then mark yourself against the rubric.

## Marking yourself

Check the answers only after you finish. Work out your raw score (out of $100$) like this:

| Item type | How it is scored |
|---|---|
| Single choice (1–6, 18) | Full marks when correct (items 1–6 are $5$ points each, item 18 is $3$ points), otherwise $0$ |
| Multiple select (7–12) | $5$ points each. Count the options you got wrong, $k$, and score $\frac{5-2k}{5}\times 5$ points; a negative result counts as $0$ |
| Fill-in (13–17) | $5$ points each, only when every cell is correct |
| Written (19, 20) | $6$ points each, criterion by criterion against the rubric |

By this rule, a multiple-select item on this paper with $k$ options judged wrong scores:

| Options judged wrong, $k$ | $0$ | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ or more |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Points | $5$ | $3$ | $1$ | $0$ |

Mark the written items honestly. The 115 Math A marking principles say: full marks need correct reasoning, a detailed process and the right answer; correct reasoning without the final answer earns partial credit; no process, a wrong inference, or data that contradicts the problem scores zero. So:

- An answer with no working scores $0$, even if the answer is right.
- Working that starts from correct premises and reasons clearly, but slips at the end, still earns the points for the earlier steps.
- Don't give yourself points for steps the rubric doesn't list.

A raw score is not a level. This is how GSAT levels <Zh>級分</Zh> are worked out: <Fact id="exam.level-scale" /> The step depends on how that year's candidates actually scored, so this paper's score cannot be turned into a level; use it to find your weak spots.
