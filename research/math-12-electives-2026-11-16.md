### C. 分科測驗 scope (CEEC 考試說明, 114學年度起適用)

- **數學甲** covers 10年級必修, 11年級數學A and 12年級數學甲 (附表一–三). CEEC says this range is unchanged from 111學年度.
- **數學乙** (new from 114) covers 10年級必修, the content shared by 11A and 11B (附表五), and 12年級數學乙 (附表六).
- Everything listed in 附表一–六, including the 說明 and 備註, is in scope. "Prerequisite knowledge and basic tools" are included automatically.
- The ※ items are recommended to be left out of national exams. For grade 12 these are the e→exp/ln remark in N-12甲-1 and the sin, cos, 2^x, 3^x derivatives in F-12甲-3.
- Some 11年級 items are excluded from 數學乙 only:
  - S-11B-1: no meridians or parallels on the sphere.
  - S-11A-1: no 三垂線定理.
  - G-11A-6: no determinants and no Cauchy inequality.
  - A-11A-1: no Cramer's rule.
  - F-11A-1: no cos or tan graphs.
  - F-11B-2: no e and no natural log.
  - D-11B-2: no link between contingency tables and Venn diagrams.
- Calculator use is set by each year's 簡章.
- Sample items tag codes such as N-12甲-1 (with A-11A-4), A-12甲-1, F-12甲-3/4/6, D-12甲-2 (with D-10-4), G-12甲-1 (with G-10-2), N-12甲-3 (with A-11A-3, F-11A-3, N-10-6), N-12乙-2 (with N-10-6), A-12乙-2, F-12乙-3/4/5, N-12乙-1 and D-12乙-2.
- I did not check whether CEEC changed anything for 115學年度 or later.

### D. Grade-12 tools that genuinely help on 學測 (10–11年級) problems

Every 學測 problem can be solved with 10–11 tools; these are checks or shortcuts only. The 10/11 codes below are as the 課綱 defines them.

1. **F-12甲-3/4 or F-12乙-3/4 derivatives → F-10-2 cubic graph features.** The 課綱 already has students expand f in powers of (x−h) to find the line the graph looks like near x=h. That line's slope is f′(h), which is exactly the polynomial Taylor expansion named in the F-12甲-4 備註. The cubic's symmetry centre is at x = −b/(3a), where f″=0. Solving f′=0 gives the turning points, which tells you how many real roots the cubic has, or how many times a horizontal line meets it. The tangent slope of y=ax²+bx+c is simply 2ax+b.
2. **Repeated roots → 餘式/因式定理 (A-10-2).** (x−a)² divides f exactly when f(a) = f′(a) = 0. A line is tangent to a polynomial curve at x=a exactly when f(x) − (the line) has a double root at a. This is correct and quick.
3. **Optimization (F-12甲-4).** It can confirm 算幾不等式 (N-10-3) answers such as the minimum of x + k/x for x>0. That needs the quotient rule or power rule for simple algebraic functions, so it is 甲 only. It can also check maxima of quadratics on a closed interval (F-10-1).
4. **N-12甲-2 / N-12乙-2 infinite geometric series → N-10-1 repeating decimals.** For example, 0.\overline{ab} = ab/100 · 1/(1−1/100) = ab/99. This justifies the 10x−x trick that N-10-1 uses informally.
5. **Σ notation (N-12-2) → N-10-6 sums and recursion.** Splitting Σ(ak²+bk+c) by linearity with the standard sum formulas is correct and fast.
6. **Sequence limits (N-12甲-1) → F-11A-3 二階轉移方陣.** The long-run steady state is the limit of Aⁿx₀. It can be found from x = Ax plus "components sum to 1". The limit concept explains why. (Steady-state wording is in 11A only.)
7. **Complex numbers in polar form (N-12甲-3) → G-11A-5 sum, difference and multiple-angle formulas, and rotations.**
   - Multiplying (cos α + i sin α)(cos β + i sin β) and comparing real and imaginary parts recovers cos(α±β) and sin(α±β). This works as a memory aid and consistency check. As a proof it is circular, because the polar multiplication rule is itself proved with the sum formulas.
   - De Moivre gives cos 3θ, sin 3θ and so on quickly.
   - Multiplying by cis θ is rotation by θ, the same as the rotation matrix in F-11A-3. This is useful for rotating a point or building a regular polygon.
   - Polar form is 甲 only.
8. **Cube roots of unity, ω with ω² + ω + 1 = 0 (A-12甲-1 / N-12甲-3) → remainder problems (A-10-2).** The remainder of f(x) divided by x²+x+1 comes from evaluating f(ω), using ω³ = 1, and matching the result to ax+b. This is correct and much faster than long division.
9. **代數基本定理 (A-12甲-1 / A-12乙-2).** A nonzero polynomial of degree n has at most n roots. This underpins "two polynomials of degree ≤ n that agree at n+1 points are equal", which is used in interpolation (A-11A-2 note) and 餘式定理 problems. The bound itself already follows from 10年級 因式定理, so the gain is modest.
10. **介值定理 (F-12-2) → 勘根.** It rigorously justifies sign-change root location for polynomial and exp/log equations, F-10-3 and F-11A-4. Note that 勘根 is mentioned only in the N-12甲-1 備註, not in the 10年級 table I saw.
11. **Random variables and the binomial distribution (D-12甲-1/2, D-12乙-1/2) → D-10-4 expected value and D-11A-2 independence.**
    - P(X=k) = C(n,k) pᵏ(1−p)ⁿ⁻ᵏ handles "exactly or at least k successes in n independent trials".
    - E = np, and linearity of expectation (split into indicator variables), give 學測 期望值 answers without listing the whole distribution.
    - The geometric distribution covers "first success on trial k", with sums in closed form using N-10-6 finite geometric series.
12. **Variance identity (D-12-1) → D-10-2 data standard deviation.** σ² = (mean of squares) − (mean)² is correct and speeds up calculations. The rule Var(aX+b) = a²Var(X) matches 10年級 standardization. The gain is small, since this is already essentially 10年級 material.
13. **Linear programming (A-12乙-1) → G-10-2 二元一次不等式 regions.** When asked for the max or min of ax+by over a polygonal region, check the vertices or slide a family of parallel lines. This is correct and helpful if 學測 asks it. I did not verify whether 學測 currently does.
14. **Inverse functions (F-12甲-1) → F-11A-4.** The graphs of 10ˣ and log x are mirror images across y=x, tying into the G-10-1 symmetry item.
15. **Circle and ellipse parametrization (G-12甲-1) → circles (G-10-3/4) and 疊合 (F-11A-2).** Write points on a circle as (h + r cos θ, k + r sin θ). Then the max or min of px+qy on the circle reduces to the 疊合 amplitude √(p²+q²)·r. This is correct, but 11A Cauchy or 疊合 already does the job.

**Weak or not useful connections:** Riemann sums and integration (F-12甲-5/6/7, F-12乙-5/6) have almost no direct 學測 use. 學測 does not ask for areas under curves, and volumes of spheres and cones are given as formulas. The conic standard forms in G-12甲-1 have no 學測 counterpart; 11B covers conics only visually.
